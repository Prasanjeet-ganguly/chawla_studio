      About the studio

      Philosophy

      We capture what you'll want to remember.

      We work quietly and in the light that is already in the room. A celebration does not need to be directed to be photographed well; it needs someone watching closely enough to see the half-second that matters, and composing carefully enough that the picture still holds up years after the day has gone soft in everyone's memory.

      Every frame on this site comes from real work — a ring ceremony, a mandap, a bride waiting to be called in. Nothing here is stock or staged.

      10+ Years of Craft

      Over a decade refining the balance of documentary observation and fine-art composition.

      True-to-Life Color

      No trendy filters. Our darkroom-inspired color pipeline preserves skin tones and rich ambient tones forever.
